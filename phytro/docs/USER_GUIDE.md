# PhyTro — user guide

*PhyTro* comes from the Greek **physikē trophē** — “natural nourishment”.

## 1. Create your account (1 minute)
1. Open the PhyTro link and click **New here? Create an account**.
2. Enter your email and a password (8+ characters, with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a number).
3. Check your email for a 6-digit code, type it in, and you're signed in.

You stay signed in on that device. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.

## 2. Tell PhyTro what matters to you (30 seconds, first visit only)
The **Profile** page opens automatically the first time:
- **Where do you usually shop?** Your default store (you can switch any time).
- **Allergies** — tap every allergen that applies. Products containing it get a red warning; "may contain traces" gets a softer one.
- **Things I avoid** — artificial dyes, artificial sweeteners, nitrites, BHA/BHT, emulsifiers, palm oil, high sugar/salt/saturated fat.
- **Diet** — vegan or vegetarian.
- **Let the AI add swaps to my list** — if on, the AI can put its recommended healthier product on your list for you.

Tap **Save**. Everything is stored in your account, so it's the same on your phone and laptop.

## 3. Search for a product by name (fastest)
Use the search box at the top of every page (or the big one on **Shop**). Type any of:
- a **name** — `cheerios`, `oat milk`
- a **brand** — `folgers`, `kirkland`
- a **description or ingredient** — `decaf coffee pods`, `peanut butter no palm oil`
- a **barcode** — the digits under the stripes (e.g. `016000275287`) jumps straight to that product

Press **Enter**. Every word you type must match the product somewhere (name, brand, category or
ingredients), so fewer words find more. Results show:
- **✓ at Walmart** — listed at the store you picked (these come first); change the store in the **Store** menu
- the PhyTro score for you, your warnings and flagged ingredients
- **Best match** / **Healthiest first** to re-sort, and **+ List** to add any result to your list

Tip: search shows products PhyTro already knows about instantly, then asks Open Food Facts for more.
If it says the database is busy, wait a minute and search again.

## 4. Find the healthiest option in an aisle
1. On **Shop**, pick your store at the top (Walmart, Target, Costco, Kroger, Whole Foods, Trader Joe's or Any US store).
2. Tap an aisle — e.g. ☕ **Coffee** or 🥣 **Cereal**.
3. The **🏆 Healthiest picks for you** are at the top. Below, every product is ranked best to worst.

Reading a product card:
| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **80–100 Great** · 🟢 **60–79 Good** · 🟠 **40–59 Fair** · 🔴 **0–39 Poor** | PhyTro score, *for you* (your allergies and avoid-list are applied) |
| Red tag like "Contains Milk (your allergen)" | A personal warning from your profile |
| Tag like "Erythrosine (FD&C Red No. 3)" | An ingredient on PhyTro's watch-list |
| "store not confirmed" | Sold in the US, but the database doesn't confirm this store carries it |

Use the filters **Good & Great only** or **No warnings for me** to shorten the list.

## 5. Look at one product
Tap a product to see why it got its score: processing level, Nutri-Score, sugar/salt per 100 g (⬆ = high),
each flagged ingredient with the reason and the regulation it comes from, and the full ingredient list as printed.

Already holding the product? Type its barcode in the search box, or tap 📷 to scan it with your phone camera
(Chrome on Android; on iPhone, type the number under the barcode).

## 6. Ask the AI agents for a deep analysis
On the product page, press **Run AI analysis**. You'll watch three agents work, live:
1. 🔎 **Label Scout** reads the raw label and pulls out the real ingredients.
2. 🧪 **Toxicology Analyst** looks up each concerning ingredient in FDA, EU and California documents.
3. 🛡️ **Shopper Advocate** double-checks the analyst, applies your profile, and finds healthier options at your store.

Each lane shows *Thinking…*, *Taking action…* (e.g. "Searching regulations: 'Red No. 3 revoked'") and *Done*. It
takes about 15–30 seconds. You then get:
- a **verdict** (Good choice / OK in moderation / Consider swapping / Avoid for you) in plain language,
- **findings with sources** — click a source to read the original document; ⚖️ notes show where sources disagree
  (for example an older rule vs. a newer ban) and which one PhyTro trusted,
- **healthier options** at the store you chose, each with a **+ List** button,
- **Run details** with the exact tokens and cost of the analysis.

The latest analysis is saved, so next time you open that product it's already there.

## 7. Build your shopping list at home, use it in the store
- Tap **+ List** on any product (aisle, product page or swap suggestion).
- **My list** groups items by store, shows your cart's average health score, and lets you tick items off as you shop.
- **Clear checked** removes what you bought; **🖨️ Print** gives a paper copy.
- Items the AI added show a 🤖 note explaining why.

## 8. Tips
- Scores only use data in the product database; "data quality: low" means the label info is incomplete.
- Change stores on the Shop page or in the AI panel to compare where the healthier swap is sold.
- PhyTro explains regulations and ingredient concerns — it doesn't diagnose or give medical advice. For allergies or
  medical diets, follow your doctor's or dietitian's guidance.
